Data Deletion Instructions

您得隨時請求芮比廣告行銷行刪除經由 Meta 應用程式「RB Advertising & Marketing」取得之資料，以及為您建立之數字人肖像與聲音資料。

You may ask RB Advertising & Marketing at any time to delete data obtained through our Meta app "RB Advertising & Marketing" and any digital-human likeness and voice data created for you.

方式一：撤銷授權 Revoke access

  1. 企業客戶：至 Meta 商家設定 →「合作夥伴」或「系統用戶」，移除「芮比廣告行銷行」或「RB Advertising & Marketing」對粉絲專頁、Instagram 及廣告帳號之存取權限。
  2. 個人用戶：至 Facebook「設定和隱私」→「設定」→「應用程式和網站」，選擇「RB Advertising & Marketing」並點選「移除」。

Businesses: Meta Business Settings → Partners / System users → remove access granted to "RB Advertising & Marketing". Individuals: Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → select "RB Advertising & Marketing" → Remove.

方式二：書面請求 Written request

來信 admin@raby.com.tw，主旨請註明「資料刪除申請」，並提供粉絲專頁／Instagram 帳號名稱、廣告帳號編號或 Brand OS 登入電子郵件，以利身分核對。

Email admin@raby.com.tw with the subject "Data Deletion Request", including your Page / Instagram name, ad account ID or Brand OS login email for verification.

處理期限 Timeline

本公司將於收到請求後 7 個工作日內回覆確認，並於 30 日內完成刪除（包括內容排程、留言紀錄、成效數據、存取權杖、數字人肖像及聲音資料），並通知技術研發方創捷同步刪除，完成後以電子郵件通知。依法令須保存之資料除外。

We confirm within 7 business days and complete deletion within 30 days (including scheduled content, comment logs, performance data, access tokens and digital-human likeness/voice data), instruct Chuang Jie, the Brand OS technology developer, to delete the same, and notify you by email—except data we are legally required to retain.
