Privacy Policy

芮比廣告行銷行（以下稱「本公司」）與創捷國際貿易股份有限公司（以下稱「創捷」）由同一創辦團隊建立，共同構成 Brand OS 生態系：創捷專注於 Brand OS AI 品牌行銷平台之核心技術研發與基礎建設；本公司為 Brand OS 之官方授權服務夥伴（Authorized Service Partner）暨首要營運夥伴，負責將平台能力導入品牌客戶之實際行銷場景。本公司於品牌策略與廣告投放之第一線實戰經驗，持續回饋至 Brand OS 之產品研發，雙方以「技術研發 × 品牌實戰」雙引擎模式協同發展。

本公司以 Brand OS 為服務之品牌客戶提供 AI 廣告圖文、商品視覺、短影音及數字人 KOL 影片之生成、管理與發佈，並透過本公司營運之 Meta 應用程式「RB Advertising & Marketing」，將經客戶核准之內容發佈至 Facebook 粉絲專頁、Instagram 及 Threads，並執行 Meta 廣告投放與成效分析（以下合稱「本服務」）。本公司謹依中華民國《個人資料保護法》及 Meta 平台政策訂定本政策，說明本服務如何蒐集、處理、利用及保護資料。

RB Advertising & Marketing ("we", "us") and Chuang Jie International Trading Co., Ltd. ("Chuang Jie") were founded by the same founding team and together form the Brand OS ecosystem. Chuang Jie focuses on the core technology and infrastructure of Brand OS, an AI brand-marketing platform. We are the Authorized Service Partner and lead operating partner of Brand OS, bringing the platform into brand clients' real-world marketing. Our front-line experience in brand strategy and advertising continuously feeds back into Brand OS product development—a "technology × execution" twin-engine partnership.

Using Brand OS, we help our brand clients generate, manage and publish AI ad creatives, product visuals, short-form videos and digital-human KOL videos. Through our Meta app "RB Advertising & Marketing", we publish client-approved content to Facebook Pages, Instagram and Threads and run and analyse Meta advertising (together, "the Service"). This policy explains how we collect, use and protect data, in accordance with Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act and Meta Platform Terms.

三、本服務蒐集之其他資料 Other data we collect

  • 客戶資料：公司名稱、聯絡人姓名、職稱、電子郵件、電話，用於契約履行、帳務及客戶服務。
  • 品牌素材：客戶提供之品牌識別、商品資訊、圖片、影片與文案，用於生成廣告圖文與影片。
  • 肖像及聲音：僅於建立數字人時，經當事人書面同意後蒐集之肖像照片與聲音樣本。
  • 系統紀錄：登入、操作及錯誤紀錄，用於資訊安全與服務品質維護。

Client contact details for contract performance and support; brand assets supplied by clients for creative generation; likeness and voice samples collected only with the individual's written consent for digital-human creation; and system logs for security and service quality.

四、AI 生成內容 AI-generated content

  • Brand OS 運用 AI 技術協助生成廣告文案、商品視覺、短影音及數字人影片。提供予 AI 服務供應商之資料，限於生成所需之品牌素材、腳本及經同意之肖像與聲音。
  • 數字人僅得以當事人本人或已取得其書面授權者之肖像及聲音建立，嚴禁冒用他人身分。
  • 所有內容於發佈前均須經客戶或本公司人員審核確認，並依 Meta 平台規範標示為 AI 生成內容。

Brand OS uses AI to help generate ad copy, product visuals, short videos and digital-human videos. AI providers receive only the brand assets, scripts and consented likeness/voice needed for generation. Digital humans may only be created from the individual's own likeness and voice or with their written authorisation. All content is reviewed by the client or our staff before publishing and labelled as AI-generated in line with Meta policies.

五、資料儲存與安全 Storage & security

  • 資料儲存於 Cloudflare 等通過國際資安認證之雲端基礎設施，傳輸全程以 HTTPS／TLS 加密。
  • Meta 存取權杖僅存放於伺服器端加密環境，不會顯示於網頁或提供予第三方。
  • 平台採帳號登入與角色權限控管，各品牌客戶僅能存取其自身資料。

Data is stored on certified cloud infrastructure such as Cloudflare and encrypted in transit with HTTPS/TLS. Meta access tokens are kept only in encrypted server-side storage. Role-based access control ensures each client can access only its own brand data.

六、保存期間 Retention

經由 Meta 取得之資料，僅於服務契約存續期間保存，於契約終止或客戶撤銷授權後 30 日內刪除；數字人肖像及聲音資料，於當事人撤回同意或契約終止後 30 日內刪除；彙總性成效報告最長保存 2 年。法令另有規定者，從其規定。

Meta data is kept only during the service contract and deleted within 30 days after termination or revocation of access. Likeness and voice data is deleted within 30 days after consent is withdrawn or the contract ends. Aggregated reports are kept for up to 2 years, unless law requires otherwise.

七、資料之提供與分享 Disclosure

除提供本服務所必要之對象外，本公司不會將資料提供予第三人。必要對象包括：技術研發方創捷（平台維運）、雲端服務（Cloudflare）、AI 生成服務（如 Anthropic、OpenAI、HeyGen 等，僅限前述生成所需資料），以及依法令或主管機關要求之情形。

We disclose data only where necessary to provide the Service: to Chuang Jie, the Brand OS technology developer, (platform operations), cloud hosting (Cloudflare), AI generation services (e.g. Anthropic, OpenAI, HeyGen—limited to the generation data described above), or where required by law.
